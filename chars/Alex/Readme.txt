=====<Move Details>=====

 - Overhaul enhances Alex's next attack with counter hit properties (more damage and more frame advantage).
 - Standing Heavy Punch spikes airborne opponents.
 - Standing Heavy Kick knocks down airborne opponents.
 - Crouching Heavy Punch launches on hit.
 - Chop and Flying Cross Chop hit overhead.
 - Flying Cross Chop causes soft knockdown.
 - Lariat forces stand.
 - Headbutt becomes Sleeper Hold upon hitting back-turned opponents.
 - Sleeper Hold causes soft knockdown.
 - Flash Chop back turns on hit.
 - EX Flash Chop causes soft knockdown.
 - Medium Slash Elbow cause soft knockdown.
 - Heavy and EX Slash Elbow launches on hit.
 - EX Slash Elbow has armor on startup.
 - Air Stampede causes hard knockdown.
 - EX Air Stampede can be piloted.
 - Power Bomb is an unblockable command grab that causes hard knockdown.
 - Light Power Bomb has the longest range, slowest startup and smallest damage; Medium Power Bomb is the middle ground;
   Heavy Power Bomb has the shortest range, fastest startup and biggest damage.
 - EX Power Bomb has Medium's range, and both faster startup and bigger damage than Heavy.
 - Spiral D. D. T. is an unblockable command grab that causes hard knockdown but only hits standing opponents.
 - Air Knee Smash is an anti-air unblockable command grab that causes hard knockdown but only hits airborne opponents.
 - EX Air Knee Smash can hit grounded opponents too, but can be guarded on startup.
 - Boomerang Raid causes hard knockdown. The final hit of the attacking sequence is an unblockable command grab.
 - Stun Gun Headbutt is an unblockable command grab that has full body projectile invincibility.
 - Stun Gun Headbutt changes jump arcs depending on the button pressed.
 - Stun Gun Headbutt causes hard knockdown. 
 - MAX Stun Gun Headbutt can be piloted and dizzies on hit.
 - Hyper Bomb is an unblockable command grab that causes hard knockdown.



=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
