Pacsonic from CA Games brings you the prototype character of Beldachi from Brawl Masters

There's a built in movelist if you're using this character in IKEMEN.
So if you're in IKEMEN, you don't need to look at this unless you don't want to pause 
your game.

-Movement-
U - up
D - down
L - left
R - right
QCF - quarter circle forward
QCB - quarter circle backwards
HCF - half circle forward
HCB - half circle back

-Basic Attacks-
a - LK/light kick
b - HK/heavy kick
x - LP/light punch
y - HP/heavy punch

-Special Moves-
Light Cartwheel - QCF, LP
Strong Cartwheel - QCF, HP
Light Backflip - QCB, LK
Strong Backflip - QCB, HK
Tornado Spin(Air) - QCB, HP

-Super-
Super Cartwheel (1 power meter) - QCF, QCF, LP+HP

-WINMUGEN-
To make her work in WINMUGEN, remove the custom AI code. Don't know why the custom AI code 
breaks WINMUGEN compatibility, but it does.
