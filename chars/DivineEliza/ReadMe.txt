----------------<Move Details>-------------------

 - Jigoku Husha can catch foes in their hit states and isn't affected by the juggle system
 - Falling Arc and air (EX)Eliza Kick are overheads
 - EX Shippuken is affected by gravity
 - (EX)Shippuken can otg
 - Joudan Yasha Gaeshi can only counter non crouch type physical attacks
 - Shoryuken Counter can counter all physical attacks
 - The starting/grounded hit of (EX)Genocide Cutter and ground (EX)Eliza Kick can not be air guarded
 - EX Eliza Kick ground bounces opponents and resets the juggle points
 - EX Jaeiken re-stands and collapses p2, allowing for extensions  
 - (MAX)Raising Storm/(MAX)Total Annihilation/Kappuru Shouryuken can not be air guarded
 - (MAX)Rashoumon is a grab and can combo from super cancels
 - Lv1 Rashoumon sets p2 up for a free juggle
 - The first hit of Kappuru Shouryuken MUST hit for the rest of the move to continue
  
---------------<Gameplay Notes>------------------

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Most Normal standing/crouching attacks can be canceled into Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Normal attacks can be command canceled into Fudou Sakkatsu Uraken
 - All Normal air attacks can be canceled into Air Eliza Kick
 - Performing a Super/MAX Cancel resets the juggle points but also reduces damage dealt
   
MAX MODE:
 - Your attack and defense is increased while in MAX Mode
 - EX moves can be performed at no power cost, though take away from time sustained in MAX mode
 - All Lv1 supers can be performed at no power cost, though immediately uses up all time in MAX mode
 - All Lv2 and higher supers will cost 1 power bar, along with using up all time in MAX mode
 - Even without a power bar, as long as you have time left in MAX mode, you can perform Lv1 supers/EX moves
 - You can not Power Charge/gain power while in MAX mode
 - You can not perform a Alpha Counter/Counter Movement while in MAX Mode
