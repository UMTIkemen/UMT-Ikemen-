This is an AI for CHOUJIN's SSJ2 Son Gohan

the AI difficulty level can be changed between 1 through 10
open the CMD file (songohan.cmd) with notepad and;


  [State -1, AIスイッチ]
  type     = VarSet
  triggerall = !var(59)
  triggerall = RoundState = 2
  trigger1 = (command="cpu1")||(command="cpu2")
  trigger2 = (command="cpu3")||(command="cpu4")
  trigger3 = (command="cpu5")||(command="cpu6")
  .
  .
  .
  trigger25 = (command="cpu49")||(command="cpu50")
  trigger26 = NumHelper(9741)
  trigger26 = Helper(9741),var(59)
  ;         AIレベル
  var(59) = 5

	   ↑change this number 


use the search option in notepad, and search for;
"AIレベル" , and it should easily be found.
