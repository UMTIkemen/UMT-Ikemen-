=====<Features>=====

 - All the essential stuff
 - Details and moves taken from his various video game appearances
 - Gameplay mixed from several games, including CvS2, KOF, SFA3 and SFIII
 - Special intros versus Kyo Kusanagi, Ash Crimson, Genjuro Kibagami and Dictator (Normal and EX modes); Kyo Kusanagi and Claw (Claw mode)



=====<Mode Overview>=====

This character has four different modes:

<Iori>
- Regular Iori Yagami
- Based primarly on his KOF '98 moveset

<Orochi Iori>
- An wild version of Iori unleashed during the Riot of the Blood
- Based primarly on his CvS2 moveset

<EX Iori>
- An alternate version of Iori, representing the more experienced version of the character as seen in the later KOF games
- Based primarly on his KOF XV moveset

<Claw Iori>
- An alternate version of Iori, representing the flameless version of the character seen in KOF XIII
- Based primarly on his flameless moveset from KOF XIII



=====<.DEF Overview>=====

This char has five different .def files, here's what each one does:

<IORI.def>
The mode is selected via palette:

Palettes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6	-> Normal Iori mode
Palettes 7, 8			-> Orochi Iori mode
Palette  9, 10			-> EX Iori mode
Palette  11, 12			-> Claw Iori mode

To add him to your Mugen, add the following line to your select.def, under [Characters]:
Iori,

=====<Movelist>=====

 U - up          x - light punch        a - light kick
 D - down        y - medium punch       b - medium kick
 F - forward     z - heavy punch        c - heavy kick
 B - back        p - any punch          k - any kick
 s - start       2p- two punches        2k- two kicks

 (Air) - Move must be performed in the air.
 (EX)  - Move has an EX version, performed by pressing two punch/kick buttons.
 (MAX) - Use two punch/kick buttons when performing a Super move to power it up.

=====<Move Details>=====

 - Ge-Shiki: Yumebiki can be command canceled from Standing Medium Punch.
 - Ge-Shiki: Gofun In "Shinigami" hits overhead.
 - Ge-Shiki: Kui hits overhead.
 - Ge-Shiki: Kui can be super canceled.
 - EX Yami Barai (Normal Mode) causes soft knockdown.
 - EX Yami Barai (EX Mode) stuns the opponent for a brief period of time.
 - LP 100 Shiki: Oni Yaki has lower body invincibility on startup.
 - MP, HP and EX 100 Shiki: Oni Yaki have full body invincibility on startup.
 - The third repetition of 127 Shiki: Aoi Hana causes hard knockdown (Orochi Mode).
 - The third repetition of EX 127 Shiki: Aoi Hana launches the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - EX 212 Shiki: Koto Tsuki In hits OTG.
 - 311 Shiki: Tsuma Kushi has projectile invincibility and can be canceled into 127 Shiki: Aoi Hana.
 - EX 311 Shiki: Tsuma Kushi ground bounces the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - Kuzukaze is an unblockable command grab.
 - Kuzukaze has full body invincibility on startup (Orochi Mode).
 - EX Kuzukaze has full body invincibility on startup.
 - 104 Shiki: Nueuchi launches the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - 401 Shiki: Shogetsu launches the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - 129 Shiki: Akegarasu can be juggled from any attack.
 - 203 Shiki: Tsuchitsubaki is an unblockable command grab that ground bounces the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - Ura 108 Shiki: Ya Sakazuki stuns the opponent for a brief period of time.
 - Ura 311 Shiki: Saku Tsuma Kushi has projectile invincibility.
 - Ura 1018 Shiki: Ya Shiori launches the opponent for a juggle reset.
 - Sanjingi no Ni is an unblockable command grab.
 - Ura 1029 Shiki: Homura Hotogi is an unblockable command grab.
 - Chi no Boso counters everything that isn't a projectile or a throw.



=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
