Jaiden Animations (Born on september 27, 1997) is a famous Aromantic American YouTuber celebrity, famously known for her comedic animated 
story-time videos and animations about her life, the games she plays and her gaming challenges. She ocassionally also does some speed-paints.
In M.U.G.E.N., she has joined the infinite tournament to prove herself as a worthy animation fighter?

:========:

======
Throws
======

Shaking
z
Jaiden grabs and shakes her opponent while she yells "Don't work your life away!" and throws her opponent.

Tennis
z
Air only
Jaiden grabs and smacks down her opponent with a Tennis racket.

=========
Mechanics
=========

Guard Crush
If Jaiden keeps blocking attacks for a time, her guard crushes, making her temporary stunned!

Parry
F
When an opponent's attack is about to make contact.
Jaiden parrys a hit from an attack, pushing her opponent back.

Guard Cancel
a+b+c
While stand guarding
Uses 500 Power
Jaiden dashes backwards, canceling guarding while being in this state when an opponent's hit makes
contact.

Get up attack
a+b+c
When hitting ground in a knockdown
Uses 500 Power
Nicole uses a knockdown move upon hitting ground in a knockdown.

Dodging
y
Both Ground and Air
Jaiden dodges the opponent's attack, becoming invulnerable.

Aerial Ground Smash
x
Air only
Jaiden raises up into the air, to launch down into a ground smash, causing heavy, knocking damage.

========
Specials
========

This Video?
D, DF, F, a/b
Jaiden magically throws thumbnails from some of her videos.
A version spawns one thumbnail, and B version spawns two.

Wow Fireworks!
F, D, DF, a/b
Jaiden takes out a firework shooter and shoots out fireworks vertically, as an anti-air.
A version does one hit, B version does two.

Kitty Slash
D, DB, B, a/b
Jaiden takes out her kitty sword and makes a downward slash, knocking down her foe, and makes another slash, causing a 
knockdown, and if close enough, a wallbounce.
A version does one downward slash, and B version does an additional slash.

===========
EX Specials
===========
*All these cost 500 of Power, and Jaiden is invincible before the attack frames.

EX This Video?
D, DF, F, c
Jaiden spawns three thumbnails, with the same properties as the Special versions.

EX Wow Fireworks!
F, D, DF, c
Jaiden shoots four fireworks causing her opponent to go higher causing a hard knockdown.

EX Kitty Slash
D, DB, B, c
Jaiden does three downward slashing still causing hard knockdowns, and ends with the same knocking slash as the Special 
versions.

======
Hypers
======

I Like This Part
D, DF,F, D, DF,F, a+b
Uses 1000 Power
Jaiden rushes down the opponent riding a skateboard, causing a squashed state, and ends with three smashes with another 
skateboard, lauching upon the last hit.

I Became Bubbles
D, DB,B, D, DB,B, a+b
Uses 2000 Power
Jaiden goes into her cheap Bubbles cosplay, and rushes into her opponent, traping it with 14 hits, slowly rising up the air 
and ends with a final uppercut.

When it Comes to the Oven
D, DF,F, D, DF,F,b+c
Uses 2000 Power
Jaiden spawns an oven, that when gets hit by the foe, it shoots out three explosions from the inside, and then explodes 
causing a big explosion pillar that causes a burnt state. If the oven is not hit, it simply opens, and dissappears.
This hyper may leave Jaiden open to attacks if the oven is not hit.

Super Kitten Slash
D, DB,B, D, DF,F,b+c
Uses 3000 Power
Jaiden takes out her kitty sword and does an eight downward slash combo, knocking her enemy down, finishing with one last 
slash, causing a wallbounce.

Anime Jaiden
D, DF,F, D, DB,B, b+c
Uses 3000 Power
Life must be less than or equal to 300
Jaiden transforms into her anime form, and makes a short ranged slash, that if the foe makes contact...
Jaiden goes into a cinematic hyper that takes place in an epic battle with a weakened opponent...

:Update changelog (07/18/2022):

Gameplay:
1. Projectiles have are now even punishable by improving their CLSNs.
2. Friction has been given when dashing.
3. Guard Crush now triggers less often.
4. Get up attack and Guard Cancel's commands were changed to be less simple.
5. Kitty Slash was made slightly slower.
6. Get up attack now can be performed in the getting up state.
7. CLSN1s from the Parries were improved.
8. Corrected chaining on Normals.
9. Added invincibility frames when starting Level 1, 2 and 3 Hypers.
10. Super Kitty Slash was made very slightly slower, and corrected voice clip placing. Also corrected guard velocities.
11. Some of the CLSNs were improved.
12. Anime Jaiden had its damage increased.
13. Crouch storng attack was improved slightly.
14. Normals can now be canceled into the super jump.
15. The smashing state on I Like This Part is now invulnerable and traspasable.
16. When it Comes to the Oven was improved slightly.
17. The Specials' startups were slightly increased in velocity.

Non-gameplay:
1. Dash effects were re-alligned.
2. Perfect graphic display codes were replaced.
3. Babality animations were fixed.
