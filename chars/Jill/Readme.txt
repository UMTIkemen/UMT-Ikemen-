Jill Specific Mechanics

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

｛Smoking recovery｝

When she uses Smoking, her meter and life are gradually restored.


｛Bash break｝

Her Bash and EX Bash can cancel out the opponent's projectiles. Furthermore, if she hits them during their attack, the opponent's hitpausetime is increased, and if the opponent is in the air, the opponent is brought down to the ground.


｛Slip wine｝

Her Slippery Wine and EX Slippery Wine puddle cannot be guarded against by the opponent. Furthermore, if the opponent steps on the puddle, the opponent falls and cannot move for a while, and in that state, the opponent cannot burst.


｛Alcoholic Power｝

She can use Recovery alcohol to recover her life and also recover all of her burst meter.
