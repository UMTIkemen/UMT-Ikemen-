  Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      

      We will use the notation:

      D - down
      F - forward
      U - up
      B - back
      
      LP - weak punch
      MP - medium punch
      HP - strong punch
      LK - weak kick
      MK - medium kick
      HK - strong kick
      PP - two punch buttons
      KK - two punch buttons
      PPP - three punch buttons
      KKK - three punch buttons

 
  ====>  EX - Move can be upgraded by pressing two punches or two kicks.

  ====>  AIR - Move can be executed in the air.


    Standard Movement
    -----------------
      U              - Jump
      UF             - Jump forwards
      F              - Walk forwards
      DF             - Crouch
      D              - Crouch
      DB             - Crouch or low guard
      B              - Walk backwards or high guard
      UB             - Jump back
      F, F           - Run
      B, B           - Back dash

    System Abilities
    ----------------
    
      MP + MK                             - V-Skill Ryusenkyaku 
      HP + HK                             - V-Trigger Shinryuken (requires 1500 power)
      PPP or KKK                          - Power Charge 


    Throws
    ------
      LP + LK Holding Fwd or Back


    Command Attacks
    ---------------

      Back + MK                              - Inazuma Kick
      Forward + HK                           - Thunder Kick
      Back + MP                              - Chin Buster
      HP (after Chin Buster 1)               - Chin Buster 2

    Special Attacks
    ----------------------

      D, DF, F, P                         - Hadoken (EX) (AIR)
      F, D, DF, P                         - Shoryuuken (EX)
      D, DB, B, P                         - Dodge Roll
      D, DB, B, K                         - Tatzumaki Senpukyaku (EX) (AIR)
 
      

    Super Attacks
    --------------------

      D, DB, B, D, DB, B, K                  - Shinpu Jinrai Kyaku (lvl 1)
      D, DF, F, D, DF, F, K                  - Shinryuken (lvl 2)
      D, DF, F, D, DF, F, P                  - Shinbu Messatsu (lvl 2)
      D, DB, B, D, DB, B, P                  - Skuhachi (lvl 3)
