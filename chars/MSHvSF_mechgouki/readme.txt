3.     Gems

      If you've ever played MSH, then you know about the Infinite Gems.
      Basically, these are different power-ups, depending on the color
      of the gem. I didn't reproduce MSH's system but, rather, I made a
      menu in which the player selects whatever gem they desire. And
      note: only Custom Mode has gems. You don't get to use them in
      Arcade Mode. Anyway, press D, DF, F, and a punch and kick of the
      same strength to select whatever gem you want. And press
      D, DB, B, and a punch and kick of the same strength to activate
      the gems (ex. D, DB, B, x+a).

      Mind Gem
         -This gem gradually fills up the power meter. Useful if
          you want to quickly gain power without doing anything.

      Power Gem
         -This is exactly what it sounds like. All of Mech Gouki's
          attacks do more damage. Also, his Hadoukens are more
          powerful.

      Reality Gem
         -While active, this gem fires different projectiles
          depending on the the strength of your attack. A weak
          punch/kick causes 3 small balls of fire to shoot out.
          A medium punch/kick will have icicles fall onto the
          opponent. And a strong punch/kick will fire homing
          electrical balls.

      Soul Gem
         -Like the Mind Gem, but instead of filling up the power
          meter, it gradually fills part of your life meter.
          Helpful if you just need that extra life to defeat an
          opponent.

      Space Gem
         -This is basically a super armor. You take less damage and
          your attacks are uninterrupt-able.

      Time Gem
         -Speed is drastically increased, including normal attacks.

4.     Button Layout:



         UB   U   UF      
                             [x]   [y]   [z]
          B   n   F          
                             [a]   [b]   [c]
         DB   D   DF
        
        ----------------------------------------

          F = forward
          B = back       x = jab     y = strong     z = fierce
          U = up         a = short   b = forward    c = roundhouse
          D = down

5.     Moves List


   --------------------------------------
   Basic Moves:
   --------------------------------------

      DASH FORWARD
         -F, F

      DASH BACK
         -B, B

      SUPER JUMP
         -D, U

      TAUNT
         -start

      GROUND THROW 1 (overhead toss)
         -F/B + y or z while standing next to opponent

      GROUND THROW 2 (kick launch)
         -F/B + b or c while standing next to opponent

      AIR THROW 1 (rolling slam)
         -F/B + y or z while next to opponent in the air

      MODE SELECTION
         -D, DF, F, start

      GEM SELECTION
         -D, DF, F, and punch and kick of same strength

      ACTIVATE GEM
         -D, DB, B, and punch and kick of same strength


   --------------------------------------
   Defensive Moves:
   --------------------------------------

      RECOVERY ROLL

      VARIABLE COUNTER

6.     Arcade Mode / Custom Mode


      *Press D, DF, F, start button to select what mode you want.


      Arcade Mode:
         -This is exactly (well, as exact as I can make it) the same
          as Mech Gouki in MSHvsSF. I tried to make him as arcade-perfect
          as possible.


      Custom Mode:
         -More combos (like being able to do a full 6-hit chain on the
          ground).
         -After tripping the opponent, he/she can still be juggled.
         -Special moves can be canceled into super moves.
         -You can recovery roll backward by pressing B, DB, D, and any
          kick button.
         -INFINITE GEMS!!! 
         -To balance him out, his attacks do less damage, and he also
          takes 25% more damage from the opponent.
