:Info:
:====:

Nicole Senicourt Watterson is one of the main characters in the Cartoon Network television series,
The incredible World of Gumball.

She is the mother figure of the Wattersons, being the mother of Gumball, Anais and the adoptive mother of Darwin, as well as
Richard's wife. She has a job at the rainbow factory. She is the most responsible of the family. She shares
physical appearance to Gumball, but not intellectually, but she has intellectual similarities with Anais as they both
share part of the character.

Nicole is almost like a typical mother; she is demanding and a hardworking person, and has a short temper as she can
get angry easily sometimes. Regardless of how much trouble they cause her, she truly loves her husband and children.

======
Throws
======

Suplex
z
Nicole grabs her foe to perform a Suplex into the ground.

Aerial Suplex
z
Air only
Nicole grabs her foe to perform an aerial suplex, works too similar to the ground version.

=========
Mechanics
=========

Guard Crush
If Nicole keeps blocking attacks for a time, her guard crushes, making her temporary stunned!

Parry
F
When an opponent's attack is about to make contact.
Nicole parrys a hit from an attack, pushing her opponent back.

Guard Cancel
a+b+c
While stand guarding
Uses 500 Power
Nicole dashes backwards, canceling guarding while being in this state when an opponent's hit makes
contact.

Get up attack
a+b+c
When hitting ground in a knockdown
Uses 500 Power
Nicole uses a knockdown move upon hitting ground in a knockdown.

Dodging
y
Both Ground and Air
Nicole dodges the opponent's attack, becoming invulnerable.

Aerial Ground Smash
x
Air only
Nicole raises up into the air, to launch down into a ground smash, causing heavy, knocking damage.

========
Specials
========

Firey Eyes
D, DF, F, a/b
Nicole spawns a fire in front of her by her eyes as she opens them, causing a burnt state to the opponent as its pushed
back.
A version spawns one fire, and B version spawns two.

Bulldog Barrage
F, D, DF, a/b
Nicole summons a wild bulldog giving aerial hits, until she lands where the bulldog goes loose and knocks up the opponent
from a bite
B version gives an additional hit, and the bulldog will have no difference from the A version.

Ballet Pirouette
D, DB, B, a/b
Nicole performs a Ballet Pirouette, knocking the opponent away, and then makes a wink pose, making her vulnerable for a
very short time as the opponent is knocked down.
A version does one hit, and B version does an additional hit.

===========
EX Specials
===========
*All these cost 500 of Power, and Nicole is invincible before the attack frames.

EX Firey Eyes
D, DF, F, c
Nicole spawns three fires, with the same properties as the Special versions causing the same burnt effect as the opponent
is pushed back.

EX Bulldog Barrage
F, D, DF, c
Nicole rises higher with the bulldog as it gives three hits, and when lands, the bulldog goes loose, with the same
properties as the regular Special versions.

EX Ballet Pirouette
D, DB, B, c
Nicole does her pirouette, giving three hits knocking down the opponent, and also makes the vulnerable pose.

======
Hypers
======

Death Stare
D, DF,F, D, DF,F, a+b
Uses 1000 Power
Nicole does a deadly stare causing an expanding wave that after some hits, it causes, either a burnt state, or the
MK toasty state, depending on what the opponent uses, if using both, it defaults to the MK toasty state.

Super Ballet Pirouette
D, DB,B, D, DB,B, a+b
Uses 1000 Power
Nicole does a hyper version of the pirouette that works similar to Ryu's Tatsumaki Senpukyaku hyper, in which it traps
the opponent in some hits from the spinning, and then launches away the opponent.

Volcano Firey Eyes
D, DF,F, D, DF,F,b+c
Uses 2000 Power
Nicole spawns a big fire pillar, that after some hits, causes the opponent to go in a burnt state.

Angry Nicole
D, DB,B, D, DF,F,a+b
Uses 2000 Power
Nicole goes into a 'berserk' state... and then rushes into the opponent to make her own version of the Midnight Bliss.
Based on episode "The Limit."

The Incredible Bulk Mom
D, DB,B, D, DF,F,b+c
Uses 3000 Power
Nicole turns into The Incredible Bulk Mom, in which it launches the opponent up when makes close contact, and makes a
super punch that traps the opponent in some hits, and gets launched spinning, causing a wall bounce.

Anime Nicole
D, DF,F, D, DB,B, b+c
Uses 3000 Power
Life must be less than or equal to 300
Nicole transforms into her anime form, and makes a short ranged punch, that if the foe makes contact...
Nicole goes into a cinematic hyper that takes place in an epic battle with a weakened opponent...
