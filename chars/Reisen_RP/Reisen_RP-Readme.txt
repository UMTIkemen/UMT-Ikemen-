Moves

X - Light Attack
Y - Medium Attack
Z - Strong Attack
A - Dash Shortcut 
B - Shortcut for Y+Z
C - Shortcut for X+Y+Z
Start - Taunt

-----------------
Command Normals
-----------------


Note: Each move indicates if it can be done on the ground only [G], in the air only [A], or both [GA]


* F,F or F+A (66 or 6A) - Forward Dash [GA] 

* B,B or B+A (44 or 4A) - Backward Dash [GA] 

* Y+Z (5yz) - Forward Throw [G] 

* B+Y+Z (4yz) - Backward Throw [G]

* F+Y (6y) - Trick Kick [G]
- Reisen performs a fakeout illusion of her 2z, but reappears in midair performing an overhead divekick.

* F+z or B+z (6z or 4z) - Super Udonge Punch [G]
- Reisen pulls back for a windup punch, reappearing some distance away from her initial position based on
  the input. Can be used to cross up the opponent under the right circumstances.

* j.D+z (j.2z) [A]
- Reisen performs a downward flip kick in midair.



-----------------
Specials & Supers
-----------------

* B,DB,D + x/y/z (412x, 412y, or 412z) - Guard Cancel Counter [G]
- Requires 1000 Power, can only be done during blockstun. Reisen counterattacks using Undersense Break with full startup invincibility.

* D,DF,F + X/Y/Z (236X, 236Y, & 236Z) - Mind Explosion [GA]
- Reisen fires a large bullet from her "gunhand", which explodes on contact. Button press determines the angle of the
  bullet. All versions slightly curve toward the opponent, and all air versions have recoil. 

* F,D,DF + X or B,D,DB + X (623X or 421X) - Ultrared Field [G]
- Reisen generates a red field on the stage. As long as the opponent is inside this field, Reisen will be invisible,
  granting her projectile invincibility. The 421 version generates the field around Reisen, while the 623 version
  generates the field around the opponent's position.

* F,D,DF + Y or B,D,DB + Y (623Y or 421Y) - Ultraviolet Field [G]
- Reisen generates a purple field on the stage. As long as the opponent is inside this field, Reisen will be have an
  copy illusion in front of her, extending the reach of her normal attacks. The 421 version generates the field around Reisen,
  while the 623 version generates the field around the opponent's position.

* F,D,DF + Z or B,D,DB + Z (623Z or 421Z) - Undersense Break [G]
- Reisen covers her eye while letting loose energy from her eyes, causing it to scatter around her. A quick melee attack
  with startup invincibility followed by upper body invincibility that best works in combos or as an anti-air reversal.

* F,DF,D,DB,B + X/Y/Z (63214X, 63214Y, & 63214Z) - Illusionary Blast [G]
- Reisen rears back and lets forth a multi-hitting beam from her eyes. The Z version aims upward.

* D,DB,B + X/Y (214X or 214Y) - Disbelief Aspect [G]
- Reisen creates a clone of herself which dissipates after firing bullets. The version used determines whether Reisen or
  her clone jumps, while the clone will always fire bullets regardless.

* D,DB,B + Z (214Z) - Spell-Breaking Drug [G] (Requires 1 Drug stock)
- Reisen tosses a glass drug vial containing an elixir that, upon successful hit, drains 1000 power from the opponent. Can
  only be used if Reisen has at least 1 drug stock available, and will gain more as she takes damage.

Spellcards
---------- 


-----------------------------
Level 1 Spellcards
-----------------------------

* D,DB,B,D,DB,B + X/Y/Z (214214X, Y, or Z) - Short Sight "X-Wave" [G]
- Reisen performs a super version of her Undersense Break, creating a purple wave of light that, on contact, creates a clone
  similar to Ultraviolet Field that lasts for 8 seconds. Has completely invincibility on startup. The Z version, however,
  causes Reisen to turn invisible for 8 seconds instead.

* D,DF,F,D,DF,F + X/Y/Z (236236X, Y, or Z) - Red Eyes "Lunacy Ray" [G]
- Reisen performs a double sweeping arc with her Illusionary Blast, hitting OTG and sweeping the opponent upwards on hit. Has
  a 3 second cooldown after using.

* D,DF,F,D,DF,F + X/Y/Z (236236X, Y, or Z) - Ricochet "Lunatic Echo" [A]
- Reisen shoots a series of waves downward from her hand. Hits OTG, but Reisen suffers a bit of recoil from use.

-----------------------------
Level 3 Spellcards
-----------------------------

* D,DF,F,D,DF,F + X+Y+Z (236236XYZ) - "Tele-Mesmerism" [G]
- Must be selected prior to match. Reisen sends out a tracing laser from the moon, tracking the opponent's position before
  firing. Holding the button will delay the firing of the laser, causing the attack to deal more hits and more damage.

* D,DB,B,D,DB,B + X+Y+Z (214214XYZ) - "Lunatic Red Eyes" [GA]
- Must be selected prior to match. Reisen lets loose several waves of energy from her eyes, which spread out to cover the
  stage. Acts as a fullscreen when used mid-screen.
