=====<Move Details>=====

 - 115 Shiki: Dokugami Mikansei has a random chance of delivering a critical hit; the critical hit version has a relatively brief period 
   of autoguard before Shingo attacks.
 - Shingo Kinsei Ore Mu-shiki, the EX version of 100 Shiki: Oniyaki Mikansei, and the EX version of 212 Shiki: Kototsuki Mikansei have
   autoguard.
 - The EX version of Shingo Kick is an overhead, as are all versions of Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Getchu, and the critical hit finisher
   of the heavy version of 101 Shiki: Oboro Guruma Mikansei.
 - The heavy version of 101 Shiki: Oboro Guruma Mikansei has a random chance of delivering a critical hit finisher.
 - Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Nie Togi can juggled afterwards with any attack; however, this can only be done once per combo.
 - The EX version of Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Nie Togi is an unblockable throw, and has the same juggle reset property as the normal 
   version.
 - The second hit of 212 Shiki: Kototsuki Mikansei can hit opponents on the ground.
 - Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Getchu can counter opponent's attacks; a successful counter will causes the opponent to bounce off the 
   opposite side wall. The EX version (if the move does not counter an attack) causes the opponent to crumple to the floor, and can be
   followed up with any attack before they hit the floor.
 - Geshiki: Kake Horin can be juggled afterwards with any attack; however, this can only be done once per combo. Additionally, it will
   cause guard crush on any opponent who blocks it. The MAX version will leave the opponent stunned in guard crush long enough to combo 
   off it. 
 - Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Kamukura is an unblockable throw.



=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
