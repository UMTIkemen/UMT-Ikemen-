Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      

      We will use the notation:

      D - down
      F - forward
      U - up
      B - back
      
      LP - weak punch
      MP - medium punch
      HP - strong punch
      LK - weak kick
      MK - medium kick
      HK - strong kick
      PP - two punch buttons
      KK - two punch buttons
      PPP - three punch buttons
      KKK - three punch buttons

 
  ====>  EX - Move can be enhanced by pressing two punches or two kicks.

  ====>  AIR - Move can be executed in the air.


    Standard Movement
    -----------------
      U              - Jump
      UF             - Jump forwards
      F              - Walk forwards
      DF             - Crouch
      D              - Crouch
      DB             - Crouch or low guard
      B              - Walk backwards or high guard
      UB             - Jump back
      F, F           - Run
      B, B           - Back dash

    System Abilities
    ----------------
    
      LP + LK                             - Throw
      MP + MK                             - Power Charge
      HP + HK                             - Time Breaker
      F  + PP                             - Roll Dodge 


    Command Attacks
    ---------------

      LP + MP                                - One Two


    Special Attacks
    ----------------------

      D, DF, F, P                         - Power Wave (EX) 
      F, D, DF, P                         - Power Charge (EX)
      D(hold), U, P                       - Rising Tackle (EX)
      D, DB, B, P                         - Burn Knuckle (EX)
      F, D, DF, K                         - Power Dunk (EX)
      D, DB, B, K                         - Crack Shoot (EX) (AIR)

    Super Attacks
    --------------------

      D, DF, F, D, DF, F, P                  - Power Geyser (EX)
      D, DF, F, D, DF, F, K                  - Buster Wolf (EX)
      D, DB, B, D, DB, B, P                  - Trinity Geyser (MAX)
