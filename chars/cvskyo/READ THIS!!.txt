This is simply a patch and fix up. Updated Warusaki3's Kyo and fixed many issues that existed in his most recent updates before his departures. (version 2011)

-- Bugs / Incorrect Stuff --

- Rekkas don't properly combo anymore (in older 2009/2010 versions, they worked just fine)
- Jumps height incorrect
- Small Jump startup (frames) too long
- S-Groove Power
- Running Speed too slow
- C-Groove Level 2 Special Cancel Nonfunctioning Error
- liedown.time incorrect... changed to proper number. this is very important because if you are doing a mixup/cross up on knockdown opponent, the timing was always off before. They don't lay down that long in CvS2.

-- Accurate CvS2 Data -- ~ Credits to JZ, JNP, JMorphman (JJJ???)
Special Thanks to JMorphman for helping me learn of the CvS2.CT table to get accurate data. Credits for JZ for creating it.

- All CLSNs and frame data fixed. With proper frame data and correct CLSNs, it actually pseudo-buffered a lot of commands. Bread n Butter combos work like they do in CvS2, and it isn't "difficult as hell" to do basic combos like c.lk --> c.lk --> c.mk --> qcf+lp/mp rekka

- Orochinagi CLSNs and "gravity" on flame shield fixed. Before if P2 hit the lvl 3/MAX orochinagi flame shield, p2 would not go high enough in the air, and would fall down way too fast. This makes it difficult to land the super on connect. Fixed that.
