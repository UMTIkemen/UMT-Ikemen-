;==========[Movelist]==========

;-----[The basics]-----

* Run: F,F
* Hop Back: B,B
* Longjump: [D],UF or [D],UB (Alternatively, jump while running)

* Launcher: c
* OTG Attack: D+c
* Meteor Smash: (in air) c
* Dash Attack: (while running) c

* Thunder Punch: F+z
* Throw: y+z or b
* Guard Cancel (500 Power): (while blocking normally) RQCB + Any Punch

;-----[Special Moves]-----

* Charge: Any Punch + c {a}

* Thunder Jolt† (1 charge): QCF Punch
>* Down: x
>* Medium: y
>* Fast: z

* Electro Ball† (1 charge): (in air) QCF Punch
>* Down: x
>* Diagonal: y
>* Forwards: z

* Quick Attack†: DP Punch
>* Forwards: x
>* Diagonal: y
>* Up: z

* Iron Tail†: QCB Punch (air OK)
>* Short: x
>* Medium: y
>* Long: z

* Thunder† (1 charge): RDP Punch
>* Close: x
>* Medium: y
>* Far: z

* Nuzzle: HCF Punch
>* Short: x
>* Medium: y
>* Long: z
=>* Refills the Charge meter!

* Discharge† (1 charge): D,D, Punch
>* Quick: x
>* Medium: y
>* Long: z
=>* Hitbox size and damage increases depending on how many charges are stored!

† - Has an EX version (costs 2 charges)

;-----[Hyper Moves]-----

* Volt Tackle (1000 Power): QCF Punch+Punch {QCF b} (Air OK)
=>* Angle changes depending on the button combination used!

* Wild Charge (1000 Power): QCB Punch+Punch {QCB b} (ground)
=>* Becomes Volt Shock Fist if used during the effects of Lightning Rod!

* Skull Bash (1000 Power): QCB Punch+Punch {QCB b} (air)

* Lightning Rod (2000 Power): D,D Punch+Punch {D,D b}
